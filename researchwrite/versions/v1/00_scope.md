# V1 scope

- Project: NWAFU_PVCarbon
- Mode: hybrid
- Text type: research plan and data requirements
- Target reader: project team and methods reviewers
- Language: zh
- Deliverable scope: county-level causal iLUC emissions, iLUC offset of PV avoided emissions, annual peak and cumulative carbon payback
- Desired version: V1, authoritative working baseline
- Constraints: use PV polygons for 2010–2022 and one continuous CLCD series; distinguish observed results from projections; do not claim county-wide carbon peaking or neutrality
- Archive after confirmation: yes; superseded technical routes are moved to `archive/obsolete_technical_routes`

## Included

1. County responsibility and territorial accounting.
2. Counterfactual induced cropland expansion.
3. iLUC carbon emissions.
4. iLUC offset ratio, observed peak year and cumulative carbon-payback year.

## Excluded from V1

1. Crop-specific yield loss and food-production compensation.
2. PV siting optimization.
3. County-wide carbon peaking or carbon-neutrality assessment.
4. Full lifecycle net-benefit accounting as a primary result.
