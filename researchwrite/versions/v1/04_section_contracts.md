# Section contracts

## Section 1: causal induced cropland expansion

- Purpose: estimate county/Phase attributable stable new cropland.
- Inputs: PV Phase, CLCD, county crosswalk, controls and validation samples.
- Allowed claims: causal only after pre-trend, intensity, distance and placebo gates pass.
- Forbidden claims: treating all observed expansion as iLUC.
- Validation: stable-first-expansion rule; non-cropland PV controls; future-treated controls; alternative ring and Phase thresholds.

## Section 2: county iLUC carbon emissions

- Purpose: convert attributable area and source land types into annual and cumulative emissions.
- Inputs: `A_induced`, prior land class, soil/biomass carbon, transition horizon and uncertainty.
- Allowed claims: responsibility-county and territorial-county accounts reported separately.
- Forbidden claims: applying one undifferentiated emission factor to all source land types.
- Validation: alternative carbon products/factors; 20/30-year horizons; Monte Carlo uncertainty.

## Section 3: offset, peak and payback

- Purpose: quantify iLUC offset of gross avoided emissions and timing metrics.
- Inputs: PV generation, marginal EF, iLUC emissions and projection scenarios.
- Allowed claims: observed peak within observation window; projected timing clearly labelled.
- Forbidden claims: county-wide carbon neutrality; post-2022 timing presented as observed.
- Validation: generation model calibration; alternative marginal EF; degradation and future-grid scenarios.
